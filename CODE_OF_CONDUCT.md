# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This is a small technical repository. The expectation is simple: engage with the
work respectfully and in good faith. Disagreement about measurements, methodology,
or code is welcome and useful — disagreement expressed through personal attacks,
harassment, or dismissiveness is not.

Concretely, do:

- Critique methods and numbers, not people
- Assume the other person is trying to get it right
- Say when you are uncertain, and show your evidence

Do not:

- Harass, insult, or demean anyone
- Post others' private information
- Derail technical discussion with off-topic conflict

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where someone
is representing this project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour by opening a private vulnerability report on this
repository (which is not public) or by contacting the maintainers through
<https://forum.groupdocs.com/>. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this document,
and may block persistent offenders.
